HISTORY
Mr. Abernathy is a 70-year-old retired electrician with right knee pain. He
reports the knee has bothered him for years and that it has become the thing
that governs his day. He cannot climb the stairs at home without pulling on the
rail, and he has moved his workbench down to the ground floor because of it.
Night pain wakes him three times per week on his own count; he has started
sleeping with a pillow between his knees.

He obtained radiographs privately before coming in, which is unusual but he was
tired of waiting. He is not registered with a primary care practice locally and
could not tell me who he last saw about the knee or when. We hold no records for
him and none have been requested; the history below is his own account, taken
today.

CONSERVATIVE TREATMENT TO DATE
None. No conservative treatment was undertaken prior to this referral. He has
not had physiotherapy, has not had an injection, has not been through a
structured strengthening programme, and has not been referred for any of these
previously. He takes naproxen from the cabinet when the knee is bad but has
never taken it as a course and could not tell me over what period or with what
effect.

He states plainly that he wishes to proceed to surgery and that he did not come
here to be sent away with exercises. I have recorded that as his position.

EXAMINATION
Gait: antalgic, right, with a shortened stance phase.
Right knee: range of motion 10 to 95 degrees. Fixed flexion deformity of 10
degrees. Varus alignment. Medial joint line tenderness. Crepitus through range.
No effusion. Ligamentously stable to varus, valgus, anterior and posterior
stress.
Left knee: range of motion 0 to 130 degrees, non-tender.

ASSESSMENT
Tricompartmental degenerative change of the right knee, symptomatic, with a
fixed flexion deformity.

PLAN
I have explained that a trial of non-operative management is expected before an
arthroplasty request will be considered, and that this is not me stalling him. He
was not pleased but has agreed to attend physiotherapy. Referral made today.
Review after that course.
