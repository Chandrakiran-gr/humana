HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS
Ms. Halloran is a 54-year-old woman presenting with low back pain radiating into
the right lower extremity. Symptoms have been present for 10 weeks. Onset was
gradual, without injury. She describes a band of pain across the low back with
radiation into the right buttock, down the lateral thigh and calf, and into the
dorsum of the right foot. She reports intermittent numbness over the top of the
right foot. Pain is worse with prolonged sitting and with forward bending, and
is partially relieved by walking. Coughing reproduces the leg pain.

She works as a school administrator and has been managing at work but is taking
frequent standing breaks.
