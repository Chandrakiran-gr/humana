PROCEDURE NOTE

Patient: Brennan, Thomas P.   MRN: 8830145
Date: 04/16/2026
Performed by: A. Reyes, PA-C, under supervision of M. Duquesne, MD

PROCEDURE: Intra-articular injection, right knee.

INDICATION: Right knee pain, medial compartment, refractory to therapy.

CONSENT: Verbal consent obtained after discussion of risks including bleeding,
post-injection flare, skin depigmentation, and subcutaneous fat atrophy at the
injection site. Patient verbalized understanding and elected to proceed. Time out
performed.

TECHNIQUE: Patient seated with the right knee flexed to approximately 80 degrees.
Superolateral approach. Skin prepped with chlorhexidine and allowed to dry
completely. Ethyl chloride applied. A 22-gauge 1.5-inch needle was advanced into
the joint without resistance. No aspirate obtained.

INJECTED: Triamcinolone acetonide 40 mg with 4 mL 1% lidocaine without
epinephrine.

TOLERANCE: Procedure tolerated well. No immediate complication. Patient reported
diminished pain within ten minutes, consistent with the local anesthetic rather
than the steroid.

POST-PROCEDURE INSTRUCTIONS: Relative rest for 24 hours. Ice 15 minutes at a
time. Expect a possible flare over the first 48 hours before benefit. Resume the
home program as tolerated. Call the office if pain is substantially worse after
48 hours.

FOLLOW UP: As previously scheduled.

A. Reyes, PA-C
