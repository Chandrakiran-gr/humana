Re: Sowande-Kristiansen, Petra. DOB 09/03/1971. MRN 6620884.

Dear Colleague,

Thank you for sending Mrs. Sowande-Kristiansen, whom I saw in clinic this
morning.

She describes pain everywhere. That is her phrase and I have not improved on it,
because when I tried to get her to localise it she said that picking a spot would
be misleading. It is worse in the mornings, worse in cold weather, and worse when
she is tired, which she usually is. It has been present for over two years and
she can date the start to the winter her mother died, though she is careful to
say she does not think that explains it.

She is a school administrator and has kept working throughout.

She has been on amitriptyline 20 mg at night for a year, which her general
practitioner started. Her view is that it helps her sleep and does nothing at all
for the pain, and she has been consistent about that across two appointments.
Nothing else has been tried.

On examination she is tender in a widespread and non-anatomical distribution.
There is no synovitis. No joint is swollen, hot, or restricted. Neurological
examination was not indicated and was not performed.

Investigations from your end are all unremarkable: inflammatory markers normal,
rheumatoid factor and anti-CCP negative, ANA negative, thyroid function normal,
vitamin D adequate.

I have explained that this pattern does not point to an inflammatory arthritis
and that I do not think further rheumatological investigation will help her. She
was disappointed and asked whether anything could be scanned. I have said I would
write to you.

She has had no imaging of the spine at any point, which she confirmed and which
matches the regional record.

With best wishes,
