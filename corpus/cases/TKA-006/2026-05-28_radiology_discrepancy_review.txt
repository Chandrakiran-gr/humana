REASON FOR REVIEW
Routine sampling. This case was drawn as part of the monthly audit of

REVIEW FINDING
The films were re-read at the meeting without reference to the original report.
The consensus reading is that the medial femorotibial joint space is preserved.
The appearance described as moderate narrowing on the original report is
attributed to obliquity on the anteroposterior projection, which foreshortens the
medial compartment and exaggerates apparent narrowing. On the lateral projection
the posterior condylar relationships are normal.

Marginal osteophytes at the medial tibial plateau are confirmed and are not in
dispute.

CONSENSUS IMPRESSION
Preserved medial femorotibial joint space. Marginal osteophyte formation at the
medial tibial plateau. Degenerative change is at most mild.

DISPOSITION
The original report is not withdrawn and remains the report of record for the
study. This review record is filed alongside it. Repeat weightbearing views with
attention to positioning are recommended before the study is relied upon for a
surgical decision.

Both documents should be read together by anyone using this study.
