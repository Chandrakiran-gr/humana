To Whom It May Concern

Re: Mrs Patricia Okonkwo-Bright, DOB 19/12/1953, MRN 6673109
Proposed procedure: right total knee arthroplasty

I write in support of the above request.

Mrs Okonkwo-Bright has suffered with her right knee for a considerable time and
has now had 18 months of failed conservative care. Every reasonable
non-operative avenue has been exhausted over that period and she has reached the
end of what can be offered short of replacement.

Her radiographs demonstrate end-stage medial compartment disease with
bone-on-bone apposition and a fixed varus deformity. The clinical picture
matches the imaging.

Functionally she is markedly restricted. She can manage roughly one block on the
flat before the pain stops her, and she now uses a cane for any community
distance. She has given up the choir on Thursday evenings because of the walk
from the car park.

On examination there is a varus deformity of the right knee with a range of
motion of 5 to 100 degrees. There is medial joint line tenderness and coarse
crepitus through range. The knee is ligamentously stable.

In my opinion she meets the criteria for arthroplasty and further delay serves no
purpose.

Yours faithfully,

H. Sundqvist, MD, FRCS
Consultant Orthopaedic Surgeon
