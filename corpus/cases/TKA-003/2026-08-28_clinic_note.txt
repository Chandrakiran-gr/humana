Clinic Note

Patient: Okonkwo-Bright, Patricia   MRN: 6673109
Date: 28/08/2026
Clinician: H. Sundqvist, MD

Mrs Okonkwo-Bright was brought forward from her scheduled appointment after
telephoning the office about her leg.

She describes three days of increasing redness and heat over the anterior and
medial aspect of the right shin and lower thigh, with the area enlarging. She
scratched an insect bite there at the weekend. She has felt generally unwell and
shivery but has not taken her temperature at home.

EXAMINATION
Temperature 37.9 C. Pulse 92. BP 132/78.
Right lower leg: confluent erythema extending from the mid-shin to approximately
four centimetres above the superior pole of the patella, warm to touch, tender,
with a demarcated advancing border which I have marked in pen and dated. The
anterior knee, which is the intended surgical field, lies within the affected
area. No fluctuance, no crepitus, no skin breach other than the excoriated bite.
No lymphangitic streaking. Inguinal nodes palpable and tender.
Knee joint itself: no effusion, no joint line warmth disproportionate to the
surrounding skin, passive range unchanged from her baseline and not
disproportionately painful. I do not think this is a septic joint.

ASSESSMENT
Active cellulitis of the right lower leg involving the anterior knee, that is,
involving the intended operative site.

PLAN
Cephalexin 500 mg four times daily commenced today, seven day course, first dose
given here. Border marked and dated. She is to photograph it daily and telephone
immediately if it advances beyond the mark, if she becomes more unwell, or if
she develops rigors.
Review in this clinic in five days to confirm resolution.

Separately: the cardiology opinion I requested for her preoperative workup has
not yet come back. That clearance remains pending and I have chased the referral
today. I cannot say anything about her fitness for anaesthetic until I have it.

H. Sundqvist, MD
