To the Medical Director
Utilization Review

Re: Mrs Delphine Achebe-Sorrentino, born 11 April 1966

I write in support of the request for single level fusion at L5-S1.

Mrs Achebe-Sorrentino has an isthmic spondylolisthesis at L5-S1 with a bilateral
pars defect. This has been present for many years and has become symptomatic
over the last period in a way that has now defeated everything short of
operative management.

She has had extensive conservative treatment over a prolonged period. This has
included a full course of supervised physiotherapy, which she completed, and
pharmacological management under her own doctor. Neither has held.

Her symptoms are low back pain with radiation into both legs. She describes it
as going down the back of both thighs, some days one side more than the other,
and she has never been able to say which leg is worse when asked. She has left
her employment as a delivery driver because she cannot sit or lift.

I have no hesitation in recommending fusion and would be grateful for a prompt
determination.

Yours faithfully

A. Ferreira-Brannigan
